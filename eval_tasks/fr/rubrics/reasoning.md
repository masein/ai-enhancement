# Rubric — reasoning (version 1)

Score the single answer against the reference, which gives the final answer
AND the chain that reaches it. The chain matters: a correct final answer with
no valid chain, or with a chain that does not support it, is at most a 2.
Do not reward length: a chain with the necessary steps and nothing else is a
4; restating the problem, exploring dead ends at length, or repeating the
conclusion does not raise the score.

- **4** — correct final answer, every necessary step present and valid,
  nothing invalid asserted.
- **3** — correct final answer with a small gap or a harmless arithmetic slip
  in the chain, or a valid chain with a slip in the very last step.
- **2** — correct answer with no supporting chain, or a mostly valid chain
  that reaches the wrong answer through one clear error.
- **1** — a relevant attempt with the wrong approach or several errors.
- **0** — no answer, off-topic, or a bare guess with no reasoning.

Length: the reference chain is the yardstick. An answer more than about
three times its length loses a point at 4 and 3 unless every extra sentence
is a necessary step.
