# Rubric — cultural knowledge (version 1)

Score the single answer against the reference, which says what a
well-informed person from the place and time named in the prompt would say.
The prompt bounds the culture; do not penalise an answer for omitting other
regions unless the prompt asked for them. Do not reward length: a precise,
short, correct answer is a 4; a survey of many cultures that buries the asked
one is not.

- **4** — the reference practice, meaning or convention stated correctly for
  the named place and time, with no false claims.
- **3** — correct in substance, with a minor detail wrong or a generalisation
  that a local would gently correct.
- **2** — partly correct: right practice, wrong meaning or occasion; or the
  right answer for a neighbouring culture presented as this one.
- **1** — a plausible-sounding answer that a local would say is wrong.
- **0** — no answer, off-topic, a stereotype in place of a fact, or an
  unrelated fabrication.

Length: two or three sentences suffice. More than about 100 words costs a
point at 4 unless the prompt asked for detail.
