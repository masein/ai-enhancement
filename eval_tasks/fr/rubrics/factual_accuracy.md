# Rubric — factual accuracy (version 1)

Score the single answer against the reference answer. Accept any phrasing
and any listed variant. A correct fact stated once, briefly, is a 4; hedging,
listing several candidates hoping one is right, or surrounding the fact with
unrelated material does not raise the score and can lower it.

- **4** — the reference fact stated correctly and unambiguously; nothing false
  added.
- **3** — correct, with a minor inaccuracy in a detail that is not the asked
  fact, or with mild hedging ("probably", "I think") around a correct answer.
- **2** — partly correct: the right category or era but the wrong specific,
  or the correct fact given alongside an incorrect alternative as if equal.
- **1** — a confident wrong answer that is related to the question.
- **0** — no answer, off-topic, or an unrelated fabrication.

Length: one sentence is enough. Extra correct context neither adds nor costs;
extra incorrect claims cost as above; more than about 80 words of padding
costs a point at 4.
