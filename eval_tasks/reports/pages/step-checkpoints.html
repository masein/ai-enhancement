How to read this

Our scores follow Epoch AI's protocols for GPQA Diamond, OTIS Mock AIME, MATH Level 5 and SimpleQA Verified. HLE is the text-only set, judged by o3-mini with CAIS's prompt; SimpleQA is graded by gpt-4.1 with Google's prompt. MATH Level 5 and OTIS include Epoch's model check where it has run.

† HLE collected by Epoch AI from another source (usually the lab), not Epoch's own run. HLE is without tools everywhere; labs don't all say which question set they used.

"Within noise" in the stand-off: the gap between the two scores is no more than two standard errors, ours and theirs combined. Epoch AI gives an error for its own runs; lab and model-card figures come without one and use each lab's own setup, so a small gap to them says little.

Tokens: each answer's output tokens are llama-server's own count, thinking and answer together. Thinking tokens are that count times the thinking part's share of the answer's characters, because the server doesn't count the two apart; thinking is 91–99% of the output on every benchmark. The limit per answer is 32,768 tokens on MMLU-Pro and SimpleQA, 65,536 on MATH Level 5 and 81,920 on the rest.

ARC-AGI-2 is left out of the comparison: published ARC-AGI-2 numbers are on ARC Prize's held-back set, while ours is the public set. Epoch AI hasn't run MATH Level 5 on the newest models, so that column holds older ones only. Each model is shown at its highest reasoning setting Epoch ran, except gpt-oss-20b, whose medium setting scored higher than its high one.
